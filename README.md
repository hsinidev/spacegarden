Spacegarden Guide



Game Overview


Spacegarden is a browser-based game where you play as an astronaut exploring a vast, procedurally generated galaxy. Your objective is to collect resources, build your spaceship, and escape the game.



How to Play


To start the game, simply open Spacegarden in your web browser. You'll see a series of screens with different environments, each containing resources and challenges. Your goal is to navigate through these environments, collecting resources and upgrading your spaceship as you go.


Controls


The controls are simple: you use the arrow keys to move your ship left or right, while the space bar allows you to jump. You can also use the mouse wheel to zoom in and out of the game screen.



Tips and Tricks for Success


To succeed in Spacegarden, focus on collecting resources efficiently. Use your ship's built-in power-ups to boost its speed or health, and prioritize resource collection over combat.


Build a sturdy spaceship with shields and armor plating to protect yourself from enemy attacks. Upgrade your ship regularly to improve its performance.

Spacegarden is more than just a simple game – it's an immersive experience that challenges you to think strategically and adapt quickly to changing circumstances. With procedurally generated levels, every time you play will be different, offering endless replay value.
